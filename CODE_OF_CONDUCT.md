# Code of Conduct

Crux contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces respectful, technically constructive, and safe for participation.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give actionable, proportionate feedback.
- Respect differing experience levels, backgrounds, and communication styles.
- Protect private information and security-sensitive reports.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to disagree constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, impersonation, and publication of another person's private information are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its participants. Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, repeated behavior, and the safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an appropriate private GitHub channel. Do not use a public issue when doing so would expose a participant or sensitive evidence.
